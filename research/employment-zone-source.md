# Development-Site Classifier Employment-Zone Source

Checked on 2026-08-01 (Australia/Sydney).

NSW Planning states that five employment zones were introduced into the Standard Instrument LEP framework, with business and industrial zones repealed from 26 April 2023 for transitioned LEPs. Current employment-zone references are **E1 Local Centre, E2 Commercial Centre, E3 Productivity Support, E4 General Industrial, and E5 Heavy Industrial**.

Sources:

- [NSW Planning — Employment zones reform](https://www.planning.nsw.gov.au/policy-and-legislation/planning-reforms/employment-zones-reform)
- [NSW Planning — Equivalent zone tables per LEP](https://www.planning.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/2023-04/equivalent-zone-tables.pdf)

Implementation decision: remove B4/B6 from the classifier’s current-zone allowlist and recognise E1–E5. Zone code alone will not qualify a listing. A development listing must also contain strong approval/development evidence and pass the residual-land-value model with sufficient stored inputs; otherwise it is retained as unqualified evidence rather than published as a development-site match.
